# NWH Lab language guideline

Apply this guideline to all future content and UI work. Korean is the primary
content language. English provides the visual and academic labels that support
the restrained, professional character of a university research laboratory.

- Use English for navigation (Home, About, People, Research, Publications, News),
  section headings, academic positions (such as Principal Investigator, Research
  Fellow, and Primary Research Subject), category labels, research interests,
  buttons, and short UI labels, including accessibility labels.
- Preserve English identity names such as NoWayHome Research Laboratory and NWH Lab.
- Use Korean for biographies, research descriptions, About and News body copy,
  accounts of events and activities, and other long-form explanations.
- Keep short academic labels separate from explanatory prose. Do not unnecessarily
  mix English terminology into Korean sentences; identity names may remain English.
- Exception: preserve the representative tagline exactly in English:
  "Exploring the unexplained behaviors of Jeong Yujong."
- Keep the document's primary language Korean (`lang="ko"`); mark English UI and
  content with `lang="en"` where appropriate for assistive technology.
- Position examples above are terminology guidance, not assigned roles or facts.
  Do not invent member information or events to populate content.
- Language-only updates must preserve the existing design and layout unless a
  design change is explicitly requested.
